=============================================================================
 SAM TAG TRACKER — CONTROLS
=============================================================================
 The same controls work in both front-ends:
   * model.py  — local OpenCV window (keyboard + mouse)
   * server.py — browser UI (buttons mirror every key; keyboard shortcuts
                 also work while the page has focus)

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 ASSIGN ATHLETES
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
   1        Next click assigns Team A athlete 1  (A1)
   2        Next click assigns Team A athlete 2  (A2)
   3        Next click assigns Team B athlete 1  (B1)
   4        Next click assigns Team B athlete 2  (B2)
   R        Next click assigns the Referee
   ESC      Cancel assignment mode
   X        Clear ALL role assignments (penalties are kept)

   How to assign: press the key (or button), then click on that person's
   box in the video. If the tracker loses someone, appearance re-ID will
   try to recover them automatically; re-assign manually if it picks wrong.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 ZONES  (fight square + tag zones)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
   D        Start drawing the FIGHT SQUARE   — click each corner in the video
   T        Start drawing TEAM A's TAG ZONE  — click each corner
   Y        Start drawing TEAM B's TAG ZONE  — click each corner
   F        Finish / close the zone being drawn
   C        Clear ALL zones
   S        Save zones to saved_points.json (also saved on shutdown)

   Editing: when not drawing, drag any corner handle to move it.
   IMPORTANT: draw the tag zones touching the fight square. Anywhere
   outside all three zones counts as the white border — an athlete who
   stands there gets a breach penalty.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 GAME / PENALTIES
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
   [        Decrease the penalty limit (game-over threshold)
   ]        Increase the penalty limit             (default: 6)
   G        Reset the game — clears penalties, game-over state, rule timers

   Penalties are recorded automatically per TEAM:
     * Double occupancy — 2 same-team athletes in the fight square > 3 s
     * Border breach    — standing outside every zone > 0.5 s
                          (includes breaches while moving between zones)
     * Quick re-tag     — tagging back in within 5 s of tagging out
   When a team reaches the penalty limit the screen turns RED (game over)
   and no further penalties are recorded until you press G.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 DISPLAY / TRACKING
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
   P        Toggle pose tracking (ankles) vs bbox tracking (bottom-centre)
   H        Toggle the on-screen help overlay
   Q        Quit (local OpenCV window only — stop server.py with Ctrl+C)

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 MOUSE (normal mode)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Left-click a person box    Assign the pending role (after 1/2/3/4/R)
   Left-click + drag corner   Move a zone corner
   (Browser UI: touch works too — tap to click, drag to move a corner)
